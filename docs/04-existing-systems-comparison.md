# 4. Existing Systems Comparison

## 4.1 Systems compared

Five systems were chosen to cover the kinds of tools a family or a specialist in Kazakhstan may meet: a public milestone app, a commercial development app, a shared baby log, a professional screening system and the national clinic system.

| ID | System | Kind | Public source |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | CDC Milestone Tracker | Free public milestone app | [cdc.gov](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones-app/index.html) (document 03, R8) |
| E2 | Kinedu | Commercial development and activity app | [kinedu.com](https://www.kinedu.com/) |
| E3 | Baby Connect | Commercial baby activity log with sharing | [baby-connect.com](https://www.baby-connect.com/) |
| E4 | ASQ Online with ASQ Family Access | Licensed online version of the ASQ-3 screening questionnaires | [agesandstages.com](https://agesandstages.com/) |
| E5 | Damumed | Medical information system of Kazakhstan polyclinics with a patient app | [damumed.kz](https://damumed.kz/) |

**Method.** The comparison uses the systems' public pages, documentation and store listings, read in October 2026. A cell says "not described" when the public sources do not mention the feature; it does not mean the feature is absent. Hands-on testing of each app (installation, about 30 minutes per app, with invented data) is a **follow-up task** before pre-defense 2.

## 4.4 Gap analysis

- **E1** is authoritative and free, but keeps the record on the parent's phone, has no growth tracking and no specialist participation.
- **E2** focuses on activities and engagement; its milestone content is the company's own.
- **E3** supports shared logging between caregivers but is not built on growth or milestone references and has no per-specialist access rule.
- **E4** is a validated screening instrument run by professional programs; it is licensed, and its scores imply screening accuracy that this project does not claim (document 03, S6).
- **E5** holds the clinic's record, but in its public description the family does not keep its own observations there and cannot share them with a speech therapist, a psychologist or a kindergarten teacher.

**Gap.** None of the five systems, in their public descriptions, combines WHO-based growth monitoring, evidence-informed milestone checklists, parent-controlled access for one specialist to one child, and attributed specialist notes in the same dated record. This is the combination the project plans to build (document 01).

## 4.5 Limitations

- Public descriptions may be incomplete or out of date; features may differ between app versions and countries.
- No system was tested hands-on yet. The results of hands-on testing will be added as a new column per system, with date and app version.
- The comparison covers features, not quality, accuracy or user satisfaction.
