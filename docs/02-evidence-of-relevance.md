# 2. Evidence of Relevance

## Status

**Interviews: 4 logged (3 parents, 1 specialist).** Four semi-structured needs interviews took place online on 23 October 2026: three parents of children aged 7 months, 1 year 8 months and 3 years 2 months (P1 to P3) and one pediatrician (SP1). The minimum of the plan is reached (3 of 3–5 parents, 1 of 1–2 specialists). The notes are in [evidence/interview-notes.md](evidence/interview-notes.md); the results are summarized in sections 2.4 to 2.6. The sample is small and qualitative (2.7).

## 2.6 Verified findings versus assumptions

- **Verified finding:** a statement supported by a logged interview (2.5), an observation record or a cited source (documents 03 and 04).
- **Assumption:** a claim that the design depends on and that has not been verified yet.

After the interviews, A1, A2, A4 and A6 are supported. A7 is supported for weight and height. A3 rests on one specialist. A8 has three supporting answers, but it is about Russian and Kazakh texts, and the language of the item was not recorded and two of the three items do not appear to be CDC 2022 items, so the answers do not test the claim fully; both are partly supported. A5 is mixed: two parents prefer a web app, one a store app, so the PWA choice (ADR-004) stays a design decision to be checked in the usability sessions. Requirements in document 06 are labelled accordingly in section 6.1a.

## 2.7 Limitations

- Three parents and one specialist are a small convenience sample; the results are qualitative and not representative.
- All interviews were online on the same day and were conducted by the two students, which may favour participants who use digital tools.
- Only one specialist (a pediatrician) was interviewed; speech therapists, psychologists and kindergarten teachers are not represented.
- For FU1 the language in which the item was read was not recorded, and two of the three items ("Walks backward", "Names at least four colors") do not appear to be items of the CDC 2022 checklists; A8 therefore remains partly supported.
- The needs interviews are not usability tests: they did not use the prototype and measured no task success (document 09, 9.2a).
- Statistics on how many families in Kazakhstan keep their own records were not found in the sources reviewed.
